Everything you expect from a real player.

YouTube's catalog, an interface built for music, and the little details that make all the difference every day.

They scroll by themselves in time with the song. The current line lights up, and tapping a line jumps playback right to it.

OFFLINE

No signal? No problem.

Download your songs and listen anywhere — on the subway, on a plane, out in the countryside.

  • Real files. Stored in Music/YouPlay, with readable names like “Artist - Title.m4a”.
  • Smart downloads. Your liked songs download automatically.
  • Offline mode. One switch, and the app only plays your downloaded songs.
  • Pick up where you left off. When you reopen the app, your song, position and queue are right where you left them.

INSTALLATION

Ready in a minute.

YouPlay installs from a single file, then keeps itself up to date.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap “Download” on your phone: the file lands in your downloads. Older (32-bit) phone? Get this version.

  2. 2

    Allow the installation

    Open the file and allow installs from this source when Android asks.

  3. 3

    Press play

    Search for a song, an artist or an album. New versions are offered right in the app.
